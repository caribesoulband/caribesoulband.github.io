01 — WHO WE ARE

A little piece of Cuba, wherever the music takes us.

CARIBE SOUL BAND brings together the vibrant spirit of Cuban music and an international live-show experience. From intimate celebrations to large-scale events, we create a warm, engaging atmosphere that gets people singing, dancing and connecting.

With performances across the Maldives, Indonesia and Poland, and more than 200 shows behind us, we know how to adapt our sound and energy to every audience.
